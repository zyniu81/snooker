Privacy Policy for Snooker App

Last updated: February 12, 2026

1. Who We Are

The controller of your personal data is the Snooker App, providing services through the maxcorp.it domain. You can contact us regarding your data at: snooker@maxcorp.it.

2. What Data We Collect

We only collect the data necessary to provide the functionality of the Snooker App:

  • Email address: For account creation, identity verification, and password recovery.
  • Username: To identify you within the game, on scoreboards, and in tournaments.
  • Profile Picture: Only if you voluntarily choose to upload one (stored via Cloudinary).
  • Technical Data: IP address and browser type (standard server logs for security purposes).

3. How We Use Your Data & Account Verification

We use your data strictly to:

  • Enable you to log in and use the application.
  • Verify your identity: Upon registration, a verification link is sent to your email. You must click this link to activate your account.
  • Save your match history, tournament results, and statistics.

Note: Accounts that are not activated via the email link within 12 hours of registration may be automatically deleted to maintain database security.

4. Third-Party Services (Processors)

We do not sell or trade your data. To provide our services, we use trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Render (USA/EU): Application hosting.
  • Neon Tech (USA/EU): Database storage for your scores and profile.
  • Cloudinary (USA/Israel): Secure storage for profile images.
  • AZ.pl (Poland): Our email server infrastructure (where snooker@maxcorp.it is hosted).

5. Cookies

We use essential "session cookies" only. These are required to keep you logged in. We do not use any tracking or marketing cookies.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have full control over your data. You have the right to:

  • Access and Correct your data: Directly via your Profile Settings.
  • Delete your account ("Right to be forgotten"): You can permanently delete your account and all associated match data at any time using the "Delete Account" button in your settings. This process is immediate and irreversible.
  • Request data portability: Contact us if you need an export of your statistics.

To exercise your rights or if you have any questions, please contact us at: snooker@maxcorp.it.
